Hard Engine Constraints — Violating These Breaks Output
RuleWhy It Matters
Never describe joint mechanics — use move name or intentEngine ignores biomechanical detail; describes intent
Spatial continuity breaks on cuts — re-anchor positionsAfter any cut, state who is where and which direction they face
≤ 3 characters tracked across cutsEngine loses tracking above 3
Exit-frame = implicit cut. No exit + re-entry in same shotCharacter leaves frame → gone for remainder of that shot
Never describe reflections (blades, puddles, mirrors)Seedance breaks scene geography on reflections
Only describe what can be seen or heard"Air smells of pine" → "Pine needles on ground, wind in branches"
Double contrast on every cut (shot size AND camera type)Single contrast cuts = invisible editing; engine ignores them
Every insert must be causally motivated, subject namedGeneric inserts render wrong content
